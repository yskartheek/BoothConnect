Voter app · concept

BoothConnect voter app

Concept screens for voters, based on spec §5.2, §7.6 and §9.2. Voters check their booth and polling-day details, share contact details if they choose, and control what their booth team can see. Every screen is shown for iOS and Android. All names and numbers are synthetic.

Sign in

Voter app · spec §5.2, §7.6
  • Voter ID number plus a one-time code to the voter's mobile.
  • The response never says whether a voter ID exists, so nobody can check someone else's registration.
  • A clear line that this isn't an official Election Commission app, which the stores' review guidelines expect.

Home

Voter app · spec §9.2
  • The most useful fact first: when and where to vote.
  • One prompt for missing details, and a way to ask the booth volunteer for help.
  • Short, approved messages from the booth team.

My details

Voter app · spec §7.6
  • Official roll data and details the voter shares are kept visibly separate.
  • The app can't change the official roll (a spec non-goal), so it points to Form 8 instead.
  • Shared details update straight away, like volunteer edits (spec v1.1).

Updates

Voter app · spec §9.2, §14
  • A plain history of everything that changed on the voter's record and every contact.
  • Transparency: the voter can see who has access, and an access log.

Campaign information

Voter app · spec §7.7
  • Voters see information campaigns only. Nothing is targeted at them using sensitive traits (spec §7.7 safeguards).
  • Filter chips by topic, and cards short enough to read at a glance.

Privacy and settings

Voter app · spec §14, DPDP Act
  • Each shared detail has its own switch. Turning one off withdraws consent and hides the value.
  • Voters can request a copy of their data or delete their account, as India's data protection law requires.

One app or two?

The spec leaves this open (§22). These screens work either as a separate voter app or as a voter mode inside the same app, chosen at sign-in. A separate app is simpler for store review and for voters.

Voter edits

Details a voter shares (mobile, language, occupation) update straight away, as with volunteer edits in spec v1.1. The official roll entry is read-only; corrections go through the Election Commission (Form 8).

Store review

  • The app says clearly that it isn't an official government app.
  • A privacy policy link, in-app account deletion, and a clear purpose for each permission.
