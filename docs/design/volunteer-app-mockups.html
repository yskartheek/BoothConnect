Milestone 1 · volunteer app · concept

BoothConnect volunteer app

Concept screens (version 2) for the Milestone 1 volunteer slice, drawn from the spec's visit flow (§7.4), navigation (§9.1), screen list (§10) and liquid-glass direction (§11). All names and numbers are synthetic. Colours and spacing are starting points for the design tokens in #53, not final visuals. Every screen is shown on both platforms: one Flutter codebase, with navigation and controls that follow each platform's own conventions. The page follows your light or dark theme.

The volunteer edits a member or records a visit and taps Save. It's stored on the phone straight away, with or without signal.

Step 2Upload automatically

Milestone 1 (basic) · spec §9.1

iOS and Android differences

  • iOS: large titles, back button with a text label, a floating glass tab bar, Add in the header, and Start visit as a bottom button.
  • Android: Material 3 top app bar, an 80dp navigation bar with a pill indicator, and floating action buttons for Add household and Start visit.
  • Both use one Flutter codebase and one set of design tokens (#55).

Reduced transparency

Following §11, glass is used only on navigation bars and key cards. When the phone's Reduce Transparency (iOS) or accessibility settings are on, or on lower-end devices, those surfaces become opaque with no blur.
